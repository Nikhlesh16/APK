Data Deletion Instructions

App: FocusModeBlocker | Updated: 2026-03-25

FocusModeBlocker stores rule and usage data locally on your device. There is no mandatory account system.

How to Delete Data

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps, then select FocusModeBlocker.
  3. Tap Storage and cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage to remove app data.
  5. Optionally uninstall the app to remove the app package and local files.

What Gets Deleted

Clearing storage removes local rule configurations, usage history stored by the app, and app preferences.
