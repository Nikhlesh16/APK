Accessibility Permission Disclosure

App: FocusModeBlocker | Updated: 2026-03-25

Why Accessibility Is Used

FocusModeBlocker uses Android AccessibilityService only to detect app context and enforce user-configured focus restrictions (for example, app blocking during active focus rules).

What Data Is Accessed

The service may process accessibility events needed to identify foreground transitions and interaction signals required for rule automation.

What We Do Not Do

We do not use accessibility data for advertising, profiling, or selling data. Data is not used to track users across apps for commercial targeting.

User Control

You can disable AccessibilityService for this app anytime from Android Accessibility settings.
