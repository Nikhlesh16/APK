Privacy Policy

App: FocusModeBlocker | Effective date: 2026-03-25

FocusModeBlocker is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not run a cloud backend for user accounts, and we do not sell personal data.

Information We Process

  • App usage metadata from Android usage access to evaluate your focus rules.
  • Accessibility events used to detect interaction patterns needed for rule automation.
  • Rule configuration data you create inside the app (for example, thresholds and schedule settings).

How Data Is Used

  • To apply your configured blocking and alert rules.
  • To show local reports and keep automation working on your device.
  • To maintain app reliability and safety behavior.

Data Storage and Sharing

  • Data is stored locally on your device in app storage.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.
  • We do not share your local usage data with third parties for advertising.

Permissions

The app may request Android permissions including:

  • Usage access, accessibility service, foreground service, notifications, vibration, and boot completed.

These permissions are required for core focus automation features.

Children's Privacy

FocusModeBlocker is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will guide removal steps.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can clear app data or uninstall the app at any time to remove locally stored rule and usage data.
